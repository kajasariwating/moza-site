A portrait in three words

Husband, father, son of a preacher man.

Read on
i.

Husband

Partner first. Two people choosing each other every day, through ordinary mornings and hard seasons alike. A home built on patience, laughter and the promise kept.

ii.

Father

The title that matters most. Present at the breakfast table, at the sideline, at the door when they come home late. Teaching by showing up, again and again.

iii.

Son

Raised between the pews and the kitchen table, on Sunday sermons and weekday grace. A preacher’s kid who carries the lessons forward — not always from the pulpit, but always by example.

“Live in such a way that the people who know you best are the ones who believe in you most.”
— Something my father taught me
